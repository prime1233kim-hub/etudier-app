About

Look beyond the surface.

Étudier was created to make fashion research more accessible to students and emerging designers. By combining visual analysis with fashion terminology and source discovery, the platform encourages users to look beyond the surface of a garment and understand the design choices behind it.

How analysis works

A multimodal AI model reads the photograph and describes silhouette, colour, garments, construction and vocabulary. It separates what is clearly visible from what is inferred, and says when it is unsure.

How sources are checked

Source research is a separate step. Links must come from real search results, and the server opens each one to confirm it works and belongs to the brand before showing it. If nothing can be confirmed, ÉTUDIER says so.

Your images

Photos you analyse are processed in memory and not stored, unless you choose to add a look to the public archive. Saved research stays in your own browser.

Limits

AI analysis can be wrong. Use it as a starting point for your own looking, and check sources before citing them.
